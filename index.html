          How to play

          1. Goal: be the last player with a hidden card. You start with 2 secret cards and some coins.
          2. On your turn, take one action. Safe ones (Income) just happen. Role actions (Tax, Steal, Assassinate, Exchange) claim a card — you don't have to actually hold it.
          3. Bluff & punish: when someone claims a role, anyone may Challenge during the timer. Caught bluffing → lose a card. Wrongly accused → the challenger loses one instead.
          4. Blocks: some actions can be blocked by claiming the right role (see the Cards page). Blocks can be challenged too.
          5. Coup (7 coins) can't be blocked or challenged. With the guess rule on, name one of the target's cards: right → they lose it, wrong → the coup fails. At 10+ coins you must coup.
          6. Timers: if nobody reacts before the bar runs out, the action simply happens and play moves on.
          7. Lose both cards and you're out — but you see everything. Last one standing wins. 🏆

          Tip: the room code stays in the top bar — friends who drop can rejoin with it.

          ALWAYS AVAILABLE

          • Income: take 1 coin. Safe — no blocks, no challenges.
          • Foreign aid: take 2 coins. Anyone may block as Duke.
          • Coup: pay 7. With the host's guess rule on, name one of the target's cards — right: they lose it, wrong: the coup fails (coins spent either way). Classic rule: the target picks a card to lose. Never blockable. Required at 10+ coins.

          Any role claim can be challenged. Prove it and the challenger loses a card; bluff and you lose one.
