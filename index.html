          Card guide

          The five roles, their actions, and what they block
          Card / Role Income / Action Counteraction (blocks)
          ◆ Duke Tax: take 3 coins. Blocks Foreign aid.
          ✦ Assassin Pay 3 coins; target loses one card. None.
          ▲ Captain Steal 2 coins from another player. Blocks a Captain's steal.
          ⬟ Ambassador Exchange: draw 2 cards, swap with your own, return 2. Blocks a Captain's steal.
          ● Contessa No action. Blocks an Assassin's attempt.

          Always available (no card needed)

          • Income: take 1 coin. Cannot be blocked or challenged.
          • Foreign aid: take 2 coins. Any player may block by claiming Duke.
          • Coup: pay 7 coins; target loses one card. Cannot be blocked. Required if you start your turn with 10+ coins.

          Any role claim can be challenged. Prove it and the challenger loses a card; bluff and you lose one.
